## The One-Line Hook
**"An AI that holds a paradox. Both answers are true."**

---

## Hero Idea (from Content Council)
**Format:** Paradox Duel — two opposing claims, argued by five voices, resolved on screen.

**Working title:** `So I asked my AI council five questions. It couldn't agree. That's the point.`

**Why it works:** Life is built on unsolvable tensions. Showing an AI *living* a paradox — not resolving it — is something no creator is doing. It's philosophical, technical, and emotional at once.
