Creative guardrails

A few constraints that keep the experiment useful instead of merely louder.

  • One surprising change per mutation.
  • Keep the original seed visible.
  • Label generated text as a concept, not a finished asset.
  • Do not imitate a living artist's exact style.
  • Keep personal, private, and identifying data out of prompts.
  • Save provenance with every gallery item.
