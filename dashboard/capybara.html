The Capybara Principle

In nature, the capybara sits still while birds perch on its back, monkeys groom it, and crocodiles share its river. It does not fight for territory — it creates territory through calmness. The organism adopts this principle: modules that are stressed don't fight each other — they soak, share gratitude, and form friendships. The result is a system where resilience comes not from strength, but from warmth.
